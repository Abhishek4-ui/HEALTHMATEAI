**SaMD Regulatory Alignment, Non-Diagnostic Disclaimers & Emergency Escalation**

## 1. Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) Stance

HealthMate AI is engineered as an **Intelligent Clinical Decision Support (CDS) and Healthcare Management System**. In accordance with:
- **Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO)** Medical Device Rules (MDR) 2017 & SaMD Guidelines,
- **National Medical Commission (NMC)** Telemedicine Practice Guidelines,
- **World Health Organization (WHO)** Guidance on Ethics & Governance of AI for Health,

HealthMate AI does **NOT** autonomously diagnose, prescribe, or treat any medical condition. All algorithmic outputs are informational screening assists that require review and confirmation by a licensed healthcare practitioner.

## 2. Standardized Clinical Safety Disclaimers

### 2.1 Medical Imaging AI Disclaimer
> **CLINICAL SAFETY MANDATE:** This algorithmic finding is an automated screening/triage assist and NOT an autonomous radiological diagnosis. Formal interpretation by a certified radiologist or licensed healthcare provider is mandatory before any clinical intervention or medication.

### 2.2 ECG & Cardiac AI Disclaimer
> **CLINICAL SAFETY MANDATE:** This automated ECG evaluation is a computational screening tool and NOT a definitive diagnosis of acute coronary syndrome or cardiac pathology. Immediate emergency evaluation (dial 112) is mandatory for chest pain, diaphoresis, or acute dyspnea.

### 2.3 Symptom Checker & Risk Assessment Disclaimer
> **CLINICAL SAFETY MANDATE:** Automated risk stratification engines estimate statistical probability based on population cohort distributions. They do not constitute clinical diagnosis. Consult a registered physician for comprehensive clinical examination.

## 3. Emergency Triage & Red Flag Protocol
- The NLP symptom classifier automatically detects acute emergency red flags (e.g. crushing chest pain, radiating arm pain, acute dyspnea, sudden numbness, altered consciousness).
- Emergency red flags override routine triage, displaying prominent warning banners with one-tap dialing to the **National Emergency Helpline 112**.
